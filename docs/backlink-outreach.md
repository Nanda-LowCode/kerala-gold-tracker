# Backlink outreach playbook

The #1 growth lever for a new (DR0, ~3-month) gold site is **backlinks**. On-page
SEO compounds slowly; links move a new domain. This is mostly *your* manual work —
link-building can't be ethically automated — but here's the kit.

## The hook asset
**`/kerala-gold-price-trends`** — an original data study (22K gold tripled +200%
since 2020). It has a CC-BY citation block and `Dataset` structured data so it's
built to be cited. Every pitch below points people to this page or a relevant
guide/calculator.

> Rule: never ask for "a backlink." Offer something useful (a stat, a data source,
> an answer). The link follows.

---

## Tier 1 — Quick, free, do these first (this week)

### Quora & Reddit answers
Search these questions and answer genuinely, citing the study/data:
- "How much has gold price increased in Kerala / India?"
- "Is gold a good investment in India 2026?"
- "What is the gold rate trend in Kerala?"
- "Where to check live Kerala gold rate?"

Answer template:
> Gold in Kerala has roughly tripled since 2020 — 22K went from ~₹4,100/g to
> ~₹13,000/g (+200%), with the steepest jump in 2024–2026. I track the daily
> AKGSMA board rate and put the year-by-year breakdown here: livegoldkerala.com/kerala-gold-price-trends.
> Hope it helps.

(Don't spam — one genuine, useful answer per relevant thread.)

### Your own Reddit community
Once the daily auto-post is live, occasionally link the study and seasonal guides
in discussion.

## Tier 2 — Blogger / journalist outreach (the real wins)

### Who to pitch
- **India personal-finance blogs** that publish "is gold a good investment",
  "gold price forecast", "gold vs FD/SIP" articles. They cite price-history data.
- **Kerala digital news desks** — Onmanorama, Mathrubhumi, Manorama Online, The
  Hindu (Kerala), Mid-Day/business desks — especially around **Akshaya Tritiya,
  Onam, Dhanteras**, when "gold price" stories run.
- **NRI / Gulf-Malayali portals** (gold matters for remittances & returns).

### Find the right person
Search `site:<publication> gold rate kerala` → find the author who covers gold →
email/Twitter. For bloggers, the contact/about page.

### Pitch template (email)
> **Subject:** Data: Kerala gold has tripled since 2020 (free to cite)
>
> Hi {name},
>
> I saw your piece on {their article topic}. I run livegoldkerala.com, where I
> track the daily AKGSMA Kerala board rate.
>
> I just published a year-by-year study showing 22K gold in Kerala has risen
> +200% since 2020 — with the sharpest jump in 2024–2026 (the yearly average
> went ~₹6,460 → ~₹14,150 in two years). Full breakdown, with methodology:
> https://www.livegoldkerala.com/kerala-gold-price-trends
>
> It's free to cite (CC BY). If it's useful for a current or upcoming gold piece,
> happy to pull any specific Kerala cut you need.
>
> Thanks,
> Nanda

### Timing
Pitch **2–4 weeks before** Akshaya Tritiya, Onam (late Aug) and Dhanteras (Nov) —
that's when editors are commissioning gold stories and want a data source.

## Tier 3 — Steady, lower-effort

- **Local business / jeweller sites & directories** — Kerala jeweller blogs,
  "best gold rate sites" listicles; ask for inclusion.
- **Regional-language portals** (Malayalam news/finance) — same pitch, translated.
- **App/tool directories** — list the calculators/site on India tool roundups.
- **HARO / Featured / Qwoted** — answer journalist requests on gold/investment.

---

## Track it
Re-check your backlink profile monthly (Ahrefs free / GSC → Links). Goal isn't
volume — it's a handful of **real, topically-relevant** links (a finance blog, a
news cite, an NRI portal) that each carry far more weight than the toxic PBN spam
already pointing at the domain (which Google ignores — no disavow needed).

## What NOT to do
- No paid link networks / PBNs / mass directory blasts — they're the exact
  pattern already poisoning the domain's profile; more would risk a penalty.
- No "link exchange" schemes at scale.
- No automated/spam outreach.
