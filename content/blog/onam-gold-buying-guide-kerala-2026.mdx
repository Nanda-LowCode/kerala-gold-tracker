Onam is the biggest gold-buying season in Kerala after the wedding months — and the time
when jewellers run their loudest "offers." This guide cuts through the marketing so you know
what's a real saving, when to buy, and whether to wait for the rate to move.

## When Is Onam 2026?

Onam 2026 peaks on **Thiruvonam — Wednesday, 26 August 2026**. The ten-day festival begins
with **Atham on 17 August 2026**, and gold buying builds through the **Atham-to-Thiruvonam**
window. Jeweller promotions usually start a few weeks earlier — often from the start of the
Malayalam month of Chingam (mid-August) — so the smart-buying window opens well before
Thiruvonam itself.

## Why Keralites Buy Gold During Onam

- **Auspicious timing.** Onam is considered a prosperous, auspicious occasion to bring gold
  into the home.
- **Bonus season.** Onam bonuses and Gulf-NRI visits put cash in hand right when the festival
  arrives.
- **Wedding prep.** The post-Onam months lead into the wedding season, so families buy bridal
  gold early.
- **Jeweller offers.** This is when making-charge discounts and gift schemes peak.

## Do Gold Rates Drop During Onam?

Not because of Onam itself. The **Kerala board rate** (set by the AKGSMA) follows
international gold prices and the rupee — it doesn't fall just because demand rises locally.
In fact, heavy festive demand can nudge premiums *up*. You can always
[check today's live Kerala gold rate here](/) and watch the
[30-day trend](/) before deciding.

What *does* change at Onam is the **making charge** — and that's where the real savings are.

## Onam Jeweller Offers — What's Real and What Isn't

Onam ads shout big numbers. Here's how to read them:

| Offer | What it really means |
|-------|----------------------|
| **"Zero / flat making charges"** | A genuine saving — making charges are 8–25% of gold value, so a waiver is real money. Confirm it applies to *your* design, not just select items. |
| **"Up to 50% off making charges"** | "Up to" usually applies to a few items. Ask the exact charge on the piece you want. |
| **Gold coin / gift with purchase** | Factor the gift's real value; sometimes the making charge is quietly higher to fund it. |
| **"Special Onam rate"** | The metal rate is the board rate — it's the same everywhere. Compare *making charges*, not the gold rate. |

Kerala's big chains — **Malabar Gold, Kalyan Jewellers, Joyalukkas, Bhima and Josco** — all
run Onam campaigns, usually built around making-charge waivers, gold-rate "advantage" schemes,
or gift coins. The metal rate they quote is the same AKGSMA board rate everyone uses, so the
only number that actually varies between them is the making charge. See our
[Kerala jeweller making-charges comparison](/jewellers) to know the typical charge ranges by
design before you weigh up two Onam offers.

## Smart Onam Buying Tips

1. **Compare making charges, not the gold rate.** The rate is uniform across Kerala; making
   charges are where shops differ by 10%+. See how the
   [Kerala board rate works](/blog/kerala-board-rate-vs-global-spot-price).
2. **Buy lightweight or plain designs** if you want the lowest making charge — intricate work
   costs more to make.
3. **Don't rush the rate.** If prices are mid-range, a making-charge waiver can save more than
   waiting for a small rate dip. Read our
   [best time to buy gold in Kerala](/blog/best-time-to-buy-gold-in-kerala) guide.
4. **Get a proper GST bill** (HSN 7113) — it protects resale and exchange value. Learn to
   [read a jewellery bill line by line](/blog/how-to-read-gold-jewellery-bill-india).
5. **Exchanging old gold?** Onam is a good time — estimate your credit first with the

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Onam a good time to buy gold in Kerala?**
Yes — for the making-charge offers, not for the metal rate (which is uniform statewide). If a
jeweller waives or cuts making charges, that's a genuine saving of 8–25% of the gold value.

**Will gold be cheaper during Onam 2026?**
The gold rate won't drop because of Onam — it tracks global prices. Your savings come from
making-charge discounts and exchange deals, not the rate.

**When do Onam gold offers start?**
Usually a few weeks before Thiruvonam (26 August 2026) — often from the start of the Malayalam
month of Chingam in mid-August — and run through Onam week. Atham, the first of the ten Onam
days, is on 17 August 2026.
