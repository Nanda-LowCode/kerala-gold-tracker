Every Kerala jeweller opens the shutter early on **Dhanteras**. Queues form before 10 AM. WhatsApp
family groups fill up with photos of coins and bangles. And most people buy on impulse, on
tradition, or on a jeweller's recommendation — three of the worst reasons to spend on gold.

This is the honest guide. Dhanteras 2026 falls on **Saturday, 8 November**, with Diwali on Monday,
10 November, and Bhai Dooj on Wednesday, 12 November. The auspicious window is small; the
opportunity to overpay is not. Here's what to actually do.

## The "Buy Gold on Dhanteras" Belief — And Its Realistic Take

The tradition says gold bought on Dhanteras **grows thirteenfold** — an offering that
prosperity itself, in the form of Lakshmi, will honour. It's a genuinely old belief, tied to
the harvest cycle: Dhanteras marked the return of merchants from long trading trips and the
Hindu New Year's household stocktake. Buying real gold on that specific day was the visible
part of a quieter act — saving, taking stock, closing books.

The modern read: **the day rewards intention, not haste**. Gold you *would have bought anyway*
in the next few months, bought early on Dhanteras with due diligence, is a well-timed
purchase. Gold bought impulsively because a WhatsApp forward said "13x return today" is
just a rushed jeweller trip with extra ceremony. There is no board rate discount on
Dhanteras; there is often a shop premium *added* because demand spikes.

If the number matters to you: check what the [Kerala board rate has done since your last
Dhanteras](/tools/gold-return-calculator). The actual return over the last 5 years is
substantial — but it's the same 5-year return regardless of which day you bought within
that year.

## What to Buy: Coins, Bars, or Jewellery?

The three main categories, honestly ranked for a Dhanteras purchase:

### 1. Gold coins (BIS 999) — the cleanest buy

- **Making charge**: 3–8%, sometimes 0% during Dhanteras promotions
- **Resale**: nearly full board rate minus a small assay fee
- **Verification**: BIS hallmark, weight stamped, sealed in tamper-proof packaging
- **What to check**: buy 5g or 8g (pavan) coins from BIS-registered outlets — Malabar,
  Kalyan, Bhima, Joyalukkas all issue their own. Bank coins (SBI, HDFC) exist but carry
  higher premiums and are non-exchangeable back.

Coins are the boring, correct answer for pure investment intent. Their value tracks the
board rate almost 1:1.

### 2. Gold bars (24K, 999) — for larger buys

- Same math as coins, better economy of scale above 20g
- Only from BIS-hallmarked refiners
- Storage matters — a 100g bar is a locker decision

### 3. Jewellery — for wearing, not investing

- Making charges: **8–25%** and this is where the money quietly leaves you
- Resale: making charge is not recoverable at all
- The 13x tradition applies to gold as a metal, not to making charges

If Dhanteras is your excuse to buy a bangle for your daughter's wedding: fine, buy the
bangle, just know you're not "investing" the making charge part. Use the [making charge
calculator](/tools/gold-making-charge-calculator) before you nod at the quoted price.

## The Purity Checklist (Do These Three Things Before Paying)

Every 916 hallmarked piece in India from 2023 onwards carries a **6-character HUID** — a
unique identifier printed alongside the BIS mark. Three checks, on the counter, before your
card taps:

1. **BIS logo present** — a small triangular mark, plus the number `916` or `750` or `999`
2. **HUID visible and readable** — six characters, alphanumeric, unique to that piece
3. **Verify the HUID via the BIS Care app** (free on Play Store / App Store) — scan or type
   the HUID; the app returns the jeweller's registration and the item's details. If the app
   says "invalid," walk out.

More: [full BIS 916 hallmark guide](/blog/gold-hallmarking-bis-916-guide).

## Three Common Dhanteras Day Shop Tricks

Peak day, tired staff, pressure from family behind you in the queue — this is when
irregularities happen. What to watch for:

**1. Making-charge inflation "just for today"**  
Some shops raise making charge percentages by 3–5% on Dhanteras, blaming demand. The metal
value is fixed by the board rate; the making charge is not. If the bracelet was 12%
yesterday it's 12% today. Don't accept "special Dhanteras rate" if it's higher.

**2. "Same-day billing shortcut" (no proper bill)**  
Some smaller outlets offer a simpler invoice on peak days — no HUID line, no separated
making charge, no explicit GST breakup. That bill is unusable for resale, exchange, or
insurance. **Insist on a full itemized bill** with HUID, per-gram rate, making charge %,
and GST separated. Every legitimate showroom will give this — some will just skip it if
you don't ask.

**3. The "matching set" upgrade**  
"You bought the earrings — take the matching bangle too, we'll give a nice rate." The rate
is the same; the making charges compound. If you didn't come to buy the bangle, don't buy
the bangle.

## Kerala-Specific Considerations

- **AKGSMA board rate is uniform statewide** — Kochi, Thrissur, Trivandrum, Kozhikode all
  pay the same per-gram number. Any "cheaper city" claim is about making charges, not the
  metal.
- **Weekend rush** — Dhanteras 2026 is a **Saturday**, so Onam-scale queues at Thrissur SN
  Park Road and Kochi Jewellery Junction. Early morning (opening hour to 10:30 AM) has the
  shortest waits.
- **Rate publishes late morning** — the AKGSMA board rate for the day usually publishes
  by 10:30 AM IST. See [how the board rate is set each morning](/blog/kerala-board-rate-vs-global-spot-price).
- **Sadhya-timing** — jewellers close 12:30–2:30 PM in many towns. Plan around it.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Dhanteras 2026 a good day to buy gold?

Yes — if you were already planning to buy gold in the next quarter, Dhanteras is an
auspicious and culturally supported window. It is not a discount day; the board rate is
the same as any other day. The "13x" claim is a belief, not a market prediction.

### Should I buy coins or jewellery on Dhanteras?

For pure investment intent, coins or bars — you get the metal at close to the board rate
with minimal making charge. For jewellery you'll wear, buy the jewellery — but understand
that 8–25% of what you pay is craftsmanship, not recoverable metal value.

### Is digital gold a good alternative for Dhanteras?

Digital gold (Paytm Gold, PhonePe Gold, MMTC-PAMP) tracks the metal price and skips making
charges, but it comes with a ~3% platform buy-sell spread and no BIS hallmark protection.
Fine for small symbolic purchases; not equivalent to physical hallmarked coin ownership.
Deeper reading: [digital gold guide](/blog/digital-gold-india-guide).

### What is the AKGSMA board rate expected to be on 8 November 2026?

Nobody knows — the AKGSMA committee sets it that morning based on international overnight
prices, the USD-INR rate, and MCX close. We'll publish the rate here as soon as it's live
that morning. Historically the day-of-Dhanteras rate is close to the surrounding week's
average; there is no consistent Dhanteras rate spike or dip in Kerala data.

### Is there GST on gold bought on Dhanteras?

Yes — the same 3% GST on the metal value and 5% GST on making charges applies every day of
the year including Dhanteras. Deeper reading: [Kerala gold tax breakdown](/blog/gold-tax-gst-kerala-2026).

### Is it OK to buy old-gold-exchange on Dhanteras?

Yes, and it can be smart. Bringing old gold reduces the new purchase's cash outlay and the
GST base. Estimate your exchange value beforehand: [old gold exchange
calculator](/tools/old-gold-exchange-calculator).
