If you're buying gold purely to invest or gift — not to wear — a **coin beats jewellery every time**: no making charges, near-pure gold, and resale close to the board rate. But "gold coin" covers everything from a bank's assay-certified bar to a jeweller's souvenir, and they're *not* equal. This is a practical ranking of your options in Kerala for 2026, judged on the things that actually matter. ## How we judge a gold coin

A "good" coin isn't about the brand on the box — it's four things:

1. **Purity** — investment coins should be **24K (999.9 / "four nines")**, not 22K. Higher purity = closer resale to the gold rate.
2. **Certification** — a **BIS hallmark + HUID** and tamper-proof, assay-certified packaging. Break the seal and resale value can drop.
3. **Buyback** — can you sell it back easily, and at what discount? A strong buyback network is worth more than a slightly lower sticker price.
4. **Premium** — the markup *over* the [live board rate](/). Every rupee of premium is a rupee you won't recover on resale, so lower is better.

## Our picks by what you're buying for

- **Best for pure investment:** an **MMTC-PAMP 24K (999.9) coin or bar**, 8g–10g. Swiss-refined, BIS-hallmarked, and the widest buyback network — the closest thing to "cash in gold form."
- **Best for gifting:** a **1g or 8g (1 pavan) 24K coin**, ideally with festive/devotional packaging. Small, certified, and universally welcome for weddings, Onam or Vishu.
- **Best for lowest cost:** compare the **premium** across a bank, a branded jeweller and an online listing on the *same day* — it varies more than people expect. A 2% difference in premium on a 10g coin is real money.
- **Avoid:** unhallmarked "gold coins," anything sold well above the board rate as a "special edition," and 22K coins if your goal is investment (buy 24K).

## Which weight should you buy?

Coins come in 0.5g, 1g, 2g, 5g, **8g (1 pavan)**, 10g, 20g and up. Smaller coins carry a slightly higher premium per gram; larger coins are more cost-efficient but less flexible to sell in parts. For most Kerala investors, **8g (1 pavan) or 10g** is the sweet spot. Use our [gram-to-pavan converter](/tools/pavan-to-gram-calculator) to see the value at today's rate.

## What you'll actually pay

Your out-the-door price is roughly:

**(board rate × weight) + premium + 3% GST.**
