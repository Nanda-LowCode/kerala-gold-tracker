## Why the exchange rate matters as much as the gold rate

Here's what many first-time NRI buyers miss: **two things move the price you pay** — the gold rate *and* the rupee.

When the rupee weakens against the dirham or dollar (as it has through much of 2024–2026), your Gulf salary quietly buys *more* Kerala gold, even if the rupee gold rate hasn't moved. A remittance sent on a day the rupee is soft stretches noticeably further at the jeweller's counter.

So if you're planning a large purchase — bridal jewellery, an investment in coins — it's worth watching *both* numbers. A dip in the rupee gold rate that coincides with a weak rupee is the sweet spot for a Gulf earner. Our homepage shows the rupee rate; a quick currency-app check on the exchange rate completes the picture.

## Buy gold in the Gulf, or back home in Kerala?

This is the eternal debate in every Gulf-Malayali WhatsApp group. There's no single right answer — it depends on *what* you're buying.

| Factor | Gulf (Dubai, Doha, etc.) | Kerala |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Metal price** | Tracks international spot closely | Official AKGSMA board rate |
| **Making charges** | Often lower, especially machine-made 21K | 8–25%, higher for handmade/temple work |
| **Common purity** | 21K (875) widely sold | 22K (916) is the standard |
| **Buyback / exchange** | May be harder once back in Kerala | Any Kerala jeweller buys 916 readily |
| **Getting it home** | Customs duty may apply on arrival | No import step |
| **Trust & familiarity** | Varies by shop | Board rate is transparent and uniform |

**Rule of thumb:** for **coins and investment bars**, the Gulf's near-spot pricing and low premiums are attractive. For **bridal and everyday jewellery you'll keep or resell in Kerala**, buying 916 at home avoids the purity mismatch and keeps buyback simple. Machine-made 21K bought in the Gulf can look like a bargain on making charges — just remember Kerala values and resells 916, so factor in the difference.

## What you'll actually pay bringing Gulf gold to Kerala

If you buy in the Gulf and carry it home, customs duty can apply above your duty-free allowance. In brief, for passengers who've lived abroad more than a year, there's a duty-free jewellery allowance (higher for women), with a customs duty on the excess; shorter stays get no free allowance.

## 21K vs 22K: the purity gap that catches NRIs out

Gulf showrooms sell a lot of **21K (875)** machine-made jewellery — light, modern, and priced keenly. Kerala, by contrast, runs on **22K (916)**, the hallmark standard for the board rate and the purity every local jeweller prices and buys back against.

That's not a problem — 21K is perfectly good gold — but it *is* a different purity, so a 21K piece won't fetch the 916 rate when valued in Kerala. If resale value at home matters to you, buy 916; if you simply want a nice piece to wear, 21K from the Gulf is fine. ## Timing it around home

Most NRI gold buying clusters around trips home and festivals — **Onam, Vishu, weddings, Akshaya Tritiya**. Those are exactly the periods when demand (and sometimes price) is highest back in Kerala. If your purchase isn't tied to a specific date, watching the rate for a few weeks beforehand usually beats buying in the festival rush.
